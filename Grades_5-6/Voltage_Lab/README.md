# Voltage Lab · Grades 5–6

[← Grade topic library](../README.md) · [Shared Voltage Lab resources](../../Shared_Resources/Voltage_Lab/)

Definitions, labels, AC/DC, power calculations and guided simulator tasks.

## How this unit works and is graded

**The packet is not homework or work over the break.** The packet contains the entire voltage unit's work, similar to our resistor unit packet. It is not assigned as homework or work over the break. We will work through it throughout the unit. The packet is one of the main graded parts of the unit and receives checkmark grades as we complete it. It will be collected at the end of the unit alongside notebook notes. Students may use their packet and notebook notes during the test.

## Notebook notes

Use Voltage Notes as a topic guide to build a useful reference for your future self. Cover the overall topics for the notes grade; you do not need to answer every prompt separately or copy every definition from the packet. Students and parents may choose the depth and format that will be most helpful.

## How to access your resources

1. Open your grade band's **worksheets and study guide** and work through the packet with our class activities.
2. Use **both** the [main PowerPoint](https://codencreateacademy.github.io/pslca-student-hub/Shared_Resources/Voltage_Lab/main-slides.html) and [supplementary PowerPoint](https://codencreateacademy.github.io/pslca-student-hub/Shared_Resources/Voltage_Lab/supplementary-slides.html). Slides open in your browser with **no account required**. Optional PowerPoint downloads are available on the shared resource page.
3. Revisit [The Engineering Mindset voltage video on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w82aSjLuD_8).
4. Use the [Voltage Lab simulator](https://pslca-voltage-lab.netlify.app/): **Practice** for learning and checking, then **Demonstrate** for the assigned screenshot evidence.
5. Use [Voltage Notes](Voltage_Notes.pdf) to organize your notebook. Bring your packet and notebook to class.
