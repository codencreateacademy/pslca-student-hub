Voltage Lab

Grades 9–10 · Definitions, measurements, formula relationships and investigations with different fixed quantities.

How this unit works and is graded

The packet is not homework or work over the break. The packet contains the entire voltage unit's work, similar to our resistor unit packet. It is not assigned as homework or work over the break. We will work through it throughout the unit. The packet is one of the main graded parts of the unit and receives checkmark grades as we complete it. It will be collected at the end of the unit alongside notebook notes. Students may use their packet and notebook notes during the test.

Notebook notes

Use Voltage Notes as a topic guide to build a useful reference for your future self. Cover the overall topics for the notes grade; you do not need to answer every prompt separately or copy every definition from the packet. Students and parents may choose the depth and format that will be most helpful.
